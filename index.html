DataPawa Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

DataPawa ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile resource management application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we access and how it is handled.

2. Data We Access

SMS Messages: To provide automatic bundle tracking, DataPawa reads SMS messages from your telecom provider (MTN, Airtel, Lycamobile). We only read messages from verified telecom senders and only from the current day. This data is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to a server.

Phone State: We access your phone state to detect your network operator (MTN, Airtel, etc.) for automatic SIM setup. We do not access your call history or contacts.

Usage Statistics: If you grant permission, DataPawa accesses Android's NetworkStatsManager to show you your data usage. This data is processed locally and never leaves your device.

Biometric Data: If you enable app lock, your fingerprint or face data is processed by Android's secure hardware. DataPawa never stores or transmits your biometric data.

3. Data Storage

All your bundle records, SIM profiles, and expenditure data are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database (AES-256 via SQLCipher). The encryption key is stored in your device's hardware-backed Android Keystore. We do not back up your data to Google Drive or any cloud service. If you uninstall the app, your data is permanently deleted.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We do not share your SMS content, usage statistics, or spending data with anyone.

5. Third-Party Services

DataPawa does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke any permission (SMS, Phone State, Usage Access, Biometrics) at any time through your phone's Settings. Revoking permissions may disable certain features.
