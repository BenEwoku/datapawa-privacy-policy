DataPawa Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

DataPawa ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile resource management application operated by Genesis Innovations (URSB-registered business in Uganda). We help Ugandan users track mobile data, airtime, SMS, and other resource balances across MTN and Airtel networks. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following data is stored only on your phone, in an AES-256 encrypted database (SQLCipher):

  • SIM profiles: The names you give your SIMs (e.g., "Work MTN", "Home Airtel")
  • Bundle records: Bundle names, quantities, prices, and expiry dates you enter manually
  • Notification history: Expiry reminders and app notifications

This data is encrypted with a key stored in your device's hardware-backed Android Keystore. It is never backed up to Google Drive or any cloud service. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.

2.2 Data Sent To Our Backend Server

When you make a purchase through the app (buying data, airtime, or other resources), we send the following to our secure backend server:

Data Purpose
Your phone number (MSISDN) To identify which account the purchase belongs to
Your order ID (a unique UUID) To prevent duplicate charges if the network is unstable
Purchase details (bundle, operator, amount) To process your purchase with MTN or Airtel
Your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token To send you purchase confirmations and expiry reminders
Transaction status To show you your purchase history in the app

2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

DataPawa does not access, collect, or transmit:

  • SMS messages — SMS reading from the app. We do not request SMS permissions.
  • Call logs — Call log access. We do not request call log permissions.
  • Contacts or calendar — Not accessed.
  • Precise GPS location — Not accessed.
  • Camera or microphone — Not accessed.
  • Bank account or card details — Payments are handled by MTN and Airtel; we never see your payment credentials.

2.4 Permissions We Request

Permission What It's For Where It's Processed
READ_PHONE_STATE Detecting your SIM operator (MTN, Airtel) for automatic SIM setup Local only
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sending you expiry alerts and purchase confirmations Local (via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Showing you how much mobile data you've used today Local only
INTERNET Communicating with our backend and with Firebase Network

2.5 Biometric Data

If you enable app lock, your fingerprint or face data is processed entirely by Android's secure hardware. DataPawa never stores or transmits your biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process purchases: Your purchase request is sent to MTN or Airtel through their APIs
  • To track your resource usage: Purchase history is stored in our backend so you can view it in the app
  • To send reminders: We notify you before bundles expire so you don't lose value
  • To prevent fraud: Idempotency keys prevent duplicate charges

4. Data Storage and Retention

4.1 Local Data

Stored on your device until you uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes it permanently.

4.2 Server Data

Purchase transaction records are stored in our secure database (hosted on Neon, a serverless PostgreSQL provider) for as long as necessary to provide the service. Records are retained for a minimum of 7 years to comply with Uganda's financial record-keeping regulations.

Your FCM token is stored only while needed to deliver notifications. It is removed when you uninstall the app or after 90 days of inactivity.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data. We share data only with:

  • MTN Uganda and Airtel Uganda — when you make a purchase, we transmit the necessary data to complete the transaction with your chosen operator
  • Neon (database hosting) — for secure storage of transaction records
  • Google Firebase — for push notification delivery
  • Law enforcement — only when legally required by Ugandan law

All third parties are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the specified service.

6. Third-Party Services

DataPawa uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — for push notifications
  • Neon — for secure database hosting
  • MTN Business API and Airtel Developer API — for purchase processing (once integrated)

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: All purchase history is visible in the app's dashboard
  • Delete your data: Uninstall the app to erase local data. To request server-side deletion, email us (see Section 9)
  • Revoke permissions: Change any permission at any time through Android Settings
  • Disable notifications: Turn off expiry alerts in the app's Settings screen
  • Lodge a complaint: Contact Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office if you believe your data has been mishandled

8. Children's Privacy

DataPawa is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, contact us to delete it.
